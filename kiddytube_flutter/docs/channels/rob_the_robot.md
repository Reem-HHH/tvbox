# Rob the Robot

- **Channel id:** `rob_the_robot`
- **Source:** Official Rob The Robot YouTube (`@Rob-The-Robot`). Single-episodes playlist is linked with **Follow off**.

## What it is

CGI preschool series: curious robot **Rob** and friends **Ema**, **TK**, and **Orbit** hop between planets to solve small problems — colors, maps, recycling, sports, and kindness.

## What’s in the app

- 36 official ~11-minute single episodes
- Farm, camping, gravity, recycling, puzzle/cheese/astronomy planets, and everyday play
- **Skipped:** Christmas / holiday specials, Halloween pumpkin packs, mummy/spooky uploads, and hour-long compilations
- **Follow off** — the official channel also has a dedicated spooky playlist and holiday specials

## Audience

Ages ~3–6. Short, bright problem-solving; good Mix-size episodes.
