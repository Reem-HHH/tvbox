# Milo

## What it is

British preschool series about Milo, a curious 5-year-old cat, with friends Lofty and Lark — role-play jobs and everyday adventures (Milkshake! / PBS Kids).

## What’s in the app

- Curated full episodes and job-theme compilations from the official channel
- Official uploads playlist linked with Follow uploads **off** by default

## Audience

Ages ~3–6. Calm storytelling; great for pretend play.
