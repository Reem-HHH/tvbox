# Miffy

- **Channel id:** `miffy`
- **Source:** Official Miffy YouTube (`@officialmiffy` / `UCTjqRlLLqGUv2fkklU-Ntiw`)

## What it is

Dick Bruna’s little white rabbit — calm picture-book stories from *Miffy’s Adventures Big & Small* (zoo, library, friends, pretend play).

## What’s in the app

- Curated official full episodes and short packs YouTube allows in embeds
- Official uploads playlist linked with **Follow on** (twice-daily sync; family title filter skips Halloween/holiday dumps)
- **Skipped:** Halloween, Easter egg hunts, ghost/wolf titles, trailers, and 2-hour dumps

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Quiet, simple, and easy to watch without much dialogue.
