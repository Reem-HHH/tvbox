# Gabby's Dollhouse

- **Channel id:** `gabby_dollhouse`
- **Source:** Official Gabby's Dollhouse YouTube (`@GabbysDollhouse` / `UC_jCXm9x_z9_kp8DNbIs6mQ`)

## What it is

DreamWorks preschool series: Gabby and the Gabby Cats explore rooms in a dollhouse — crafts, songs, pretend play, and gentle problem-solving.

## What’s in the app

- Curated official full episodes, songs, and toy-play packs YouTube allows in embeds
- Official uploads playlist linked with **Follow on** (twice-daily sync; family title filter skips Halloween/holiday dumps)
- **Skipped:** Halloween / holiday dumps, movie trailers, Roblox, and other-show crossovers

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Bright, magical, and very accessible.
