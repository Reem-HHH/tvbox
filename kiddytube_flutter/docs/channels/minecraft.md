# Minecraft (Dad & Olivia)

- **Channel id:** `minecraft`
- **Source:** Games For Kids Hub — official **Dad & Olivia Play** playlist (`PLCGF5P4ZzZ6d2S5-RVKKkFp-wLZk-uKP9`), plus curated calm family builds

## What it is

A dad and child (Olivia) build together in Minecraft—treehouses, fish houses, zoos, cabins, farms. Calm co-op play rather than dramatic Minecraft stories.

## What’s in the app

- Hand-picked **Dad & Olivia** and gentle family build starters (treehouse, fish house, zoo, cabin, chicken coop, sheep farm, villager shops).
- Linked to the **Dad & Olivia Play** playlist only — **not** the full Games For Kids Hub uploads (those include scary biomes, combat, and other games).
- Follow uploads **off** by default.

## Audience

Ages ~4–8. Fine for a 4-year-old who likes watching quiet building; less “story drama” than typical Minecraft YouTube.
