# Sesame Street

- **Channel id:** `sesame`
- **Source:** Official Sesame Street YouTube (`@SesameStreet`). Full-episodes playlist is linked with **Follow off**.

## What it is

Classic US preschool series: Elmo, Cookie Monster, Abby, Big Bird, and friends — letters, numbers, kindness, and street stories.

## What’s in the app

- 33 official single full episodes (~26 min)
- Everyday play, farm, camping, dentist, counting, and songs
- **Skipped:** Halloween, Christmas/holiday, Valentine, New Year, gingerbread, multi-hour compilations, and Mecha Builders / talk-show extras
- **Follow off** — the official full-episode playlist also dumps holiday specials

## Audience

Ages ~2–6.
