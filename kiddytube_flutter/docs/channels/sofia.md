# Sofia the First

- **Channel id:** `sofia`
- **Source:** Official Disney Jr YouTube (`@disneyjr`). Sofia playlist is linked with **Follow off**.

## What it is

Disney Junior series: ordinary girl **Sofia** becomes a princess when her mother marries the king. Kindness, school, siblings, and a talking amulet — plus a newer **Royal Magic** spin with castle school.

## What’s in the app

- Origin special *Once Upon a Princess*
- Season 1 full episodes (~22 min), skipping Christmas / witch-titled stories
- Two *Royal Magic* full episodes (*Welcome to Charmswell*, *Abracadazzle*)
- **Skipped:** *Holiday in Enchancia*, Halloween songs, *The Little Witch*, Miss Nettle, and mixed Mickey/Bluey compilations
- **Follow off** — the official playlist is mostly clips and holiday dumps

## Audience

Ages ~3–7. Gentle royal-school stories; some magic / sorcerer plots.
