BabyBus preschool safety cartoon: Sheriff Labrador and Officer Bellman solve small mysteries while teaching road safety, manners, and everyday habits.

## What’s in the app

- Curated full cartoon episodes from the official channel (safety lessons, detective stories, good habits)
- Official uploads playlist linked with **Follow on** (twice-daily sync; Shorts and lives skipped)
- **Skipped:** Shorts, live streams, and podcasts

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Educational pretend-play; some episodes are “catch the mischief” mysteries, not scary.
