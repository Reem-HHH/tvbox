# افتح يا سمسم (Iftah Ya Simsim)

- **Channel id:** `sesame_ar`
- **Source:** Official **أهلا سمسم** YouTube channel (`@ahlansimsim`). Season 1 playlist is linked with **Follow off**.

## What it is

The Arabic Sesame Street — **افتح يا سمسم** — with نعمان، غرغور، كعكي and friends. Letters, numbers, neighbors, and everyday play in Arabic.

## What’s in the app

- Season 1 full episodes 1–27 (~22–27 min) from the official channel
- **Follow off** — the same channel also hosts أهلا سمسم, songs, and shorts

## Audience

Ages ~3–8. Arabic-first preschool.

| Dimension | Assessment |
