# Masha and the Bear

- **Channel id:** `masha`
- **Source:** Official English Masha and The Bear channel + curated episode starters
- **Follow uploads:** off

## What it is

Non-dialogue slapstick preschool comedy: curious Masha and a patient bear. Classic episodes (e.g. Recipe for Disaster, Laundry Day) focused on play and kitchen mess—not culture-war themes.

## What’s in the app

Verified official starters including Recipe for Disaster and family-friendly episode collections.

## Audience

Ages ~3–7. Mild cartoon chaos; fine for most preschoolers with parent judgment.
