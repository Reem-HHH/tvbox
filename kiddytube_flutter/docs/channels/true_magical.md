# True's Magical Adventure (True and the Rainbow Kingdom)

- **Channel id:** `true_magical`
- **Source:** Official True and the Rainbow Kingdom YouTube (`@TrueandtheRainbowKingdom`). Series playlist is linked with **Follow off**.

## What it is

Guru Studio / Netflix Jr preschool series: **True** and her cat **Bartleby** use wishes to help friends in the Rainbow Kingdom. Tile name matches how families often search it (“True’s Magical Adventure”); the show title on YouTube is **True and the Rainbow Kingdom**.

## What’s in the app

- 28 official full episodes (~22 min) from seasons 1–3
- Includes *Wish Gone Wild* / Magical Friends–era stories
- **Skipped:** Halloween / Tricky Treat, Easter / Wuzzle Wegg, Valentine’s / Happy Hearts Day, shorts, and multi-hour compilations
- **Follow off** — official uploads also dump crafts, shorts, and holiday specials

## Audience

Ages ~3–6. Colorful, gentle problem-solving; some loud wishes / slapstick.
