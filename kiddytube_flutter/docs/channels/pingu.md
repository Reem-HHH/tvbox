# Pingu

- **Channel id:** `pingu`
- **Source:** Official Pingu YouTube channel uploads (`@pingu` / `UCM88mtSE0zRTn5ae4EbYcuw`)

## What it is

Classic nonverbal penguin slapstick—easy for toddlers who do not share a spoken language with the show.

## What’s in the app

- Official uploads playlist linked with Follow uploads **off**
- Curated starter cartoons from the official channel

## Audience

Ages ~2–5.
