# Peppa Pig

- **Channel id:** `peppa`
- **Source:** Official Peppa Pig YouTube uploads playlist

## What it is

Global preschool cartoon about Peppa’s family—everyday preschool life, play, and mild mischief.

## What’s in the app

- Official **uploads playlist** (large; synced when online)
- No separate curated starter list in seed

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Very accessible; some parents find the personality/“cheeky” tone annoying rather than unsafe.

## Rating / clean / conservative

| Dimension | Assessment |
