# Twirlywoos

- **Channel id:** `twirlywoos`
- **Source:** Official WildBrain uploads playlist + starter episode IDs

## What it is

CBeebies / Ragdoll preschool series: four curious bird-like characters, gentle slapstick, and everyday concepts (soft, directions, joining, hiding). Lower stimulation than most YouTube kids compilations.

## What’s in the app

- Uploads playlist: official Twirlywoos - WildBrain (`UC6-m1hdh8xEu-XBJK3v1TPg` → `UU…`)
- Seed starters include Soft, This way that way, Joining Up, Sneaking in the Kitchen, Full, Connecting, Turning, Going Over, and Full (Kid Movies)
- Turn on **Follow uploads** in Parent settings (remote-friendly ON/OFF button) to pull more episodes on sync

## Audience

Ages ~2–4. Good calm-discovery option beside Sarah & Duck.
