# Toy Kitchen

- **Channel id:** `toy_kitchen`
- **Source:** Curated YouTube video list

## What it is

Pretend kitchen / play-food unboxing and playset demos. Not a licensed cartoon series—kept as its own show tile so it is not dumped into a “Playtime” mix.

## What’s in the app

- Travel Kitchen Playset Unboxing
- Kitchen Set & Toy Fruits

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Everyday pretend play.
