## What it is

Arabic preschool series (**In the Night Garden** / حديقة المرح)—gentle bedtime-style stories with Igglepiggle, Upsy Daisy, and friends. Previously aired on Baraem / Ajyal.

## What’s in the app

Curated short full episodes from WildBrain Arabic: نظيف نظيف، جوجو، الطائرة الظريفة، أواني الزهور، الدمى المضحكة، plus a few gentle bedtime clips.

## Audience
