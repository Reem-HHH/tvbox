# بابار (Babar)

- **Channel id:** `babar`
- **Source:** Curated Treehouse Direct / Babar & Badou episodes on YouTube
- **Follow uploads:** off (allowlist only)

## What it is

Classic Babar the Elephant cartoons plus gentler **Babar and the Adventures of Badou** preschool episodes. Arabic full-episode uploads on YouTube are mostly unofficial, so the seed uses reputable English Treehouse Direct episodes that are family-safe and complete.

## What’s in the app

Expanded Treehouse Direct allowlist (~18 episodes): classic Babar (City Ways, City of Elephants, Elephant Express, Best Friend, Between Friends, Monkey Business, Remember When, siblings, dinner with Rataxes, helping hands, what’s mine) plus Badou double-bills (Kite Fight, Brave Guy, Thunderclap, Spy Trap, Ruby Rumpus, Unhidden Courtyard, Fair is Fair).

## Audience

Ages ~3–7.
