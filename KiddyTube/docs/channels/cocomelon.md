# CoComelon

- **Channel id:** `cocomelon`
- **Source:** Official CoComelon uploads playlist + curated nursery-rhyme starters
- **Follow uploads:** off

## What it is

English preschool nursery rhymes (wheels on the bus, bath song, farm songs). No dialogue-driven social messaging—simple songs and routines.

## What’s in the app

Verified starters: Wheels on the Bus, Bath Song (+ compilation), Old MacDonald, school / birthday songs from the official channel.

## Audience

Ages ~1–5.
