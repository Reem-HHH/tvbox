# Maruko Chan (ちびまる子ちゃん)

- **Channel id:** `maruko`
- **Source:** Official 【公式】ちびまる子ちゃんねる uploads + curated starters
- **Follow uploads:** off

## What it is

Classic Japanese slice-of-life anime about Sakura Momoko (“Maruko”) and everyday school/family moments in Showa-era Japan. Gentle comedy, music clips, and light cooking shorts from the official channel.

## What’s in the app

- Theme-song starters (おどるポンポコリン openings/endings) and a dance ending clip
- A classic play episode clip (ジャンケンポン) and two cooking shorts
- Official **uploads playlist** linked with Follow uploads **off** by default — enable only after parent review (feed includes trailers and merchandise)

## Audience

Ages ~4–8. Dialogue is Japanese; fine for kids who enjoy the music/visuals or are learning Japanese with a parent.
