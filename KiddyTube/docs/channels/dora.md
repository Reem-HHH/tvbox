# Dora the Explorer

- **Channel id:** `dora`
- **Source:** Official Dora & Friends YouTube uploads (`@DoraOfficial`)

## What it is

Preschool adventure series about Dora, her monkey friend Boots, Map, and **Backpack** (حقيبة الظهر)—the “girl and her bag” show often remembered from Spacetoon’s Arabic dub. Interactive problem-solving and friendly exploration.

## What’s in the app

- Official **uploads playlist** synced when online (can include trailers, shorts, live streams, or non-episode uploads—review after sync).
- No hand-picked starter video list in seed (library comes from playlist sync).

## Audience

Ages ~2–5. Classic preschool pacing; official channel is English-heavy (Spanish words appear). Arabic Spacetoon dubs are not auto-selected unless you paste a different playlist.
